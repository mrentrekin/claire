Addiction Support Ministry

For anyone struggling with drugs or alcohol, and for the people who love them. No one should walk this alone.

WednesdaysWeekly evening meetingCall for time & place

Come find love, strength and hope among people who understand. The ministry was begun by parishioners Maureen and Joe Vitulli and takes its spirit from a prayer of St. Teresa of Ávila about being Christ's hands for one another.
