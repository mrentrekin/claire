Youth Ministry

For teens from after Confirmation through senior year. Bring a friend.

How to plug in

  1. Join the Flocknote group
    All gathering details and sign-ups go out by email and text.
  2. Come to the next gathering
    Dinner is provided, so sign up so we can plan.
  3. Serve
    Events like the parish BBQ count toward school service hours.
