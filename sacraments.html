Who is eligible to prepare?

Reconciliation & Eucharist

  • Baptized in a Catholic church
  • Completed 1st-grade level preparation and enrolled in 2nd-grade level, in Catholic school or parish Faith Formation
  • Registered parishioners of St. Clare of Assisi

Confirmation

  • Baptized in a Catholic church, with Reconciliation and Eucharist received
  • Completed 3rd–6th grade level preparation and enrolled in 7th-grade level
  • Registered parishioners of St. Clare of Assisi

Child not enrolled in Catholic school or Faith Formation yet? Call the office and we will find the right starting point.
