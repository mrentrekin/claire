Simon Ministry

Like Simon of Cyrene, we help carry the load: keeping eleven parish buildings in good repair.

Recent projects

  • Gibbstown Rectory: boiler replaced, flooring replaced, mold and asbestos remediation
  • St. Joseph Church: bat remediation, cleaning and ceiling repair
  • Parish Hall: window replacements (Catholic Strong project)
  • Paulsboro Convent: plumbing, electrical and HVAC work

Handy with tools, or willing to learn? See the Parish Improvement Mission or volunteer.
